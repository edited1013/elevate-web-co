About Me

A local kid building something real.

I’m the person behind Elevate Web Co., based in Western New York. My background is in manufacturing, quality assurance, and CAD, where one missed detail can ruin the whole job. I bring that same standard to every website.

I started Elevate because I love building things and solving problems, and because so many great local businesses are still held back by outdated sites or agency prices they can’t justify. I’m building this company from the ground up, and my mom has been in my corner the whole way. That’s the kind of support I want to give every owner I work with.

My goal is simple: help local businesses look professional, load fast, and make it easy for customers to call, book, or ask for a quote.

When you hire Elevate, you’re hiring the person who designs and builds your site. No middlemen, no runaround.
