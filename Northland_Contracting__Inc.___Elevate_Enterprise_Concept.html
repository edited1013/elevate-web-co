Concrete Flatwork & Slabs

Warehouse Floors, Parking Decks & Heavy Slabs

Industrial and commercial facilities demand exceptionally flat, durable slab floors. Northland Contracting utilizes advanced laser screeding and surface finishing techniques to deliver high Ff/Fl floor flatness numbers for distribution centers, manufacturing plants, and commercial buildings.

Placement & Professional Finishing

High-Tolerance Surface Treatments & Hardener Applications

Our skilled finishing crews oversee slump testing, strategic placement, screeding, bull floating, troweling, and liquid hardener application. We maintain stringent quality control from truck discharge to final curing compound application.

Power Trowel Finishing Liquid Surface Hardeners Curing & Sealing
