Division 01 // Earthmoving, Grading & Subsurface Preparation

Excavation & Site Grading

VERIFIED SCOPE: Professional excavation, earthmoving, positive slope grading, and site preparation for properties across Dunkirk, NY and surrounding towns.

Proper excavation is the physical anchor of any successful property project. Without precise subgrade elevation and controlled drainage, even the finest landscaping will suffer from standing water and seasonal erosion.

01

Positive Lot Grading & Runoff Control

Reshaping depressed lawns or problematic low spots where rainwater collects, establishing smooth, consistent slopes away from foundations and walkways.

02

Site Clearing & Pad Prep

Stripping topsoil, removing stubborn overburden, and cutting level subgrades ahead of driveways, outbuildings, or outdoor living spaces.

03

Trenching & Drainage Lines

Controlled trench excavation for surface water redirection, downspout discharge routing, and yard drainage improvements.

Part A // Landscaping Services

Clean Ground Cover, Bed Definition & Yard Renewal

Once rough grading is settled, landscaping turns raw soil into an orderly, maintainable outdoor environment tailored to the lot’s natural light and drainage.

  • Topsoil Spreading & Finish Lawn Prep: Screened soil placement, fine raking, and preparation for new turf establishment.
  • Mulch, Stone & Bed Reshaping: Defined planting borders, decorative aggregate beds, and clean transitions along foundations.
  • Overgrown Lot & Brush Cleanup: Reclaiming neglected yard perimeters and restoring clean sightlines across residential grounds.

Part B // Property Improvement

Practical Structural & Functional Exterior Enhancements

Beyond seasonal yard care, property improvement bridges the gap between earthwork and everyday usability—improving access, durability, and visual order.

  • Driveway & Gravel Pad Refreshing: Re-grading, aggregate top-dressing, and stabilizing access lanes or equipment parking areas.
  • Hardscape & Border Transitions: Walkways, stone edging, and clean patio or seating-area ground preparation.
  • Exterior Property Restoration: Comprehensive multi-step cleanups and physical site upgrades tailored to owner goals.
