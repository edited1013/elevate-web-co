Where Every Detail Matters.

A gown you love should feel like it was made for you.

Goldsworthy Alterations & Design helps brides and clients arrive at the perfect fit through careful craftsmanship and personal service. From wedding dress alterations to everyday garments that deserve a better fit, each piece is approached with patience and respect for the design you chose.

Fittings are unhurried. We listen first, then pin, adjust and refine until the garment sits exactly as it should.

Before your fitting.

Bring your wedding shoes

Shoe height changes how a hem falls, so bring the exact pair you’ll wear on the day to every fitting.

Wear your planned undergarments

Fit depends on what’s underneath. Decide the plan early and bring the same pieces each time.

Bring accessories and a reference photo

A veil, jewelry or a picture of the look you want helps us shape the details around your vision.

Book early for busy seasons

Wedding season fills quickly, so we suggest reaching out as soon as you have your gown.

Let’s talk about your garment.

Tell us what you have in mind and when you need it. Bridal fittings are best booked early.
