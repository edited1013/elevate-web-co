About Onix Barbershop

More Than a Haircut. It's Your Signature Look.

Since opening our doors in 2019 at 404 Central Avenue, Onix Barbershop has stood as a cornerstone of modern barbering and community pride in Dunkirk, New York.

We blend traditional straight-razor discipline with contemporary fade techniques. Whether you speak English or Español, you'll step into a clean, relaxed, and respectful shop where every client leaves looking sharp and feeling confident.
