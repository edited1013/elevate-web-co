Our Heritage

Four Generations of Family Tradition in Dunkirk

The story of Mary's Deli runs deep in the heart of Dunkirk. It all began when the building housed a grocery store operated by Rose and Samuel Lima. In 1979, Mary transformed her parents' grocery store into the warm, inviting pizzeria it is today.

Currently operated by Kevin Russo, the restaurant proudly maintains four generations of family presence within these walls. We believe in treating every customer who walks through our doors like family—serving up hearty portions, authentic homemade sauce, and dough prepared fresh every single day.
